Hotter pan, longer life: water can float on its own steam, and a block of dry ice can spin a turbine. Here is the Leidenfrost effect, and the lab experiment that turned it into an engine.

In this video:
- Why a drop of water lasts longer on a hotter pan
- The vapour cushion under the drop
- The 2015 dry ice "sublimation heat engine"
- A hot ball in water, and liquid nitrogen on a desk
