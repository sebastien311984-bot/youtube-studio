## Description

Could you survive lost at sea? Three real castaways say yes: Steven Callahan (76 days, 1982), Poon Lim (133 days, 1942-43) and José Salvador Alvarenga (about 438 days, 2012-14). We drop you in a life raft and use animation to show the rule of threes, how dehydration works, why seawater kills (osmosis), how to get fresh water, and how ocean currents carried Alvarenga roughly 10,000 km across the Pacific.

In this video:
- The rule of threes
- Why you can't drink the sea
- Three men, three answers
- How to make fresh water
- The lottery of rescue
