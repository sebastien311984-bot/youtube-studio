FAQ
Can you outrun lava? Usually yes, most flows move at walking pace or slower.
What is a pyroclastic flow? A fast avalanche of hot gas, ash and rock, often over 100 km/h.
What should you do in an eruption? Leave early when authorities say, avoid valleys, wear a mask against ash, and never go to watch.
