FAQ
How fast is a tsunami? About 800 km/h (500 mph) in deep ocean, much slower near shore.
Can you outrun a tsunami? Do not bet on it: go uphill or inland instead.
What are the warning signs? A strong or long earthquake, a sudden sea retreat, or a loud ocean roar.
Is the first wave the biggest? Not always. Stay high until officials say it is safe.
