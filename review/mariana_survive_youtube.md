FAQ
How deep is the Mariana Trench? Challenger Deep is about 10,935 meters deep; surveys differ by a few dozen meters.
What is the deepest scuba dive? About 332 meters, by Ahmed Gabr in the Red Sea in 2014, followed by almost 14 hours of decompression.
How much pressure is at the bottom? About 1,100 times the pressure at the surface.
Who has been to the bottom? Jacques Piccard and Don Walsh in 1960, James Cameron in 2012, Victor Vescovo and others from 2019.
What is the deepest fish? Snailfish have been filmed at about 8,000 meters and a bit deeper.
