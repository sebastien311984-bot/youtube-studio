FAQ
How many people survived the Titanic? About 710 of roughly 2,224 aboard.
How cold was the water? About -2°C (28°F).
How many lifeboats did the Titanic have? Twenty, with room for about 1,178 people.
How long did the Titanic take to sink? About 2 hours and 40 minutes after hitting the iceberg.
Which ship rescued the survivors? The Carpathia, around 4:10 AM.
