FAQ
Can sharks sense a heartbeat? They detect weak electric fields from muscles and heart at close range, roughly a meter.
How strong is a great white bite? A 2008 model estimated about 18,000 newtons in a very large shark.
How many sharks do humans kill? A 2013 study estimated about 100 million a year (63 to 273 million).
