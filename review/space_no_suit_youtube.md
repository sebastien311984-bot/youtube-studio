FAQ
How long can you survive in space without a suit? You stay conscious about 15 seconds; recovery may be possible if pressure returns within about 90 seconds.
Does your blood boil in space? No, it stays under pressure in your vessels, though water in tissues vaporizes.
Would you freeze instantly? No, heat loss is slow in a vacuum.
