What happens to your body as it freezes? From 37 C to 13.7 C: shivering at 35, confusion at 33, shivering stops near 30, paradoxical undressing, terminal burrowing, heart arrhythmia near 28, and the real story of Anna Bagenholm, who survived a core temperature of 13.7 C in 1999.

FAQ
Why do freezing people take their clothes off? Paradoxical undressing: tired blood vessels open and the skin feels hot.
What does 'not dead until warm and dead' mean? Cold bodies can be revived, so doctors rewarm before declaring death.
