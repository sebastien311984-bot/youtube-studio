Could you survive falling into Saturn? No, but the journey down is unlike anything else in the solar system. Saturn has no surface, its rings are slowly raining into the planet, a hexagon wider than Earth sits at its north pole, and winds reach about 1,800 km/h. FAQ
Could you land on Saturn? No. It is a gas giant with no solid surface.
What is the hexagon on Saturn? A six-sided jet stream around the north pole; each side is wider than Earth.
Are Saturn's rings disappearing? Scientists estimate ring material is falling into the planet and the rings could be gone in a few hundred million years; the exact timescale is debated.
How did Cassini end? On 15 September 2017 it was sent into Saturn's atmosphere on purpose, transmitting data until the end.
