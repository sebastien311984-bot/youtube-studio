FAQ
Can you survive a lightning strike? Yes. In the US, roughly 90% of people struck survive, though injuries can be lasting.
Is a car safe in lightning? Yes, because of its metal shell, not its tyres.
Does lightning strike twice? Yes. The Empire State Building is hit about 20 to 25 times a year.
What is the 30-30 rule? If thunder follows a flash within 30 seconds, go indoors, and wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.
