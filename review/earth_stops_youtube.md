FAQ
Could Earth really stop spinning? No known process could do it suddenly. The spin only slows gradually, mainly through tidal friction with the Moon.
How fast does Earth spin? About 1,670 km/h at the equator, less at higher latitudes, near zero at the poles.
How fast is Earth slowing? The day lengthens by roughly 1.7 to 2.3 milliseconds per century.
