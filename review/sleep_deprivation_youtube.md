FAQ
How long can a person stay awake? The best-known documented experiment is 264 hours (Gardner, 1963-64); it is dangerous and not recommended.
What is a microsleep? A lapse of a fraction of a second up to about 30 s when the brain briefly sleeps.
Does sleep clean the brain? In mice, the space between cells widens ~60% in sleep and waste clears faster (Xie 2013).
