Could you survive Apollo 13? In April 1970 an oxygen tank exploded 55 hours into the flight, and three astronauts had 87 hours to get home with a crippled ship, little power, little water and rising carbon dioxide. Follow the mission clock, hour by hour, from the explosion to the splashdown.

FAQ
What caused the Apollo 13 explosion? Oxygen tank 2 in the service module failed after damaged wire insulation sparked inside it (NASA Cortright report).
What was the mailbox? A CO2 filter adapter built on board from a plastic bag, cardboard, a spacesuit hose and duct tape, to fit square command module canisters to round lunar module sockets.
How far from Earth did Apollo 13 get? About 400,171 km, a distance record set in 1970.
When did Apollo 13 splash down? 17 April 1970, near Samoa, recovered by USS Iwo Jima.
